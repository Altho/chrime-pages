TOUT SUR LA GUITARE BLUES

Published
January 1, 2013
Publisher
Electric or acoustic guitar
Share
  • Cultura →
  • La flute de pan.com →

This book will help you: Master most basic blues rhythm patterns in all keys.

Enrich and solidify your vocabulary when improvising over blues progressions.

Construct your own blues progressions.

Learn dozens of famous riffs and create your own. Discover guitarists you may not have known before who have nonetheless shaped music history. You can use the backing tracks included with this book to practice playing the rhythm patterns and turnarounds. You can also use them for your own improvisation practice, including in musical styles other than the blues. The various parts are performed on the CD; for most of the excerpts, you will also find the backing track used to record those specific parts.
